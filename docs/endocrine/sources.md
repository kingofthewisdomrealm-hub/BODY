| Source | What we took | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| RadiologyInfo, [Thyroid scan and uptake](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/thyroiduptake) | Nuclear function vs ordinary x-ray | **Place** |
| Cleveland Clinic, [Thyroid uptake and scan](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diagnostics/thyroid-uptake-scan) | RAIU vs scan; hyperthyroid use | **Place** |
| RSNA *RadioGraphics*, Hoang et al., [The thyroid: imaging features](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/rg.342135067) | US first for nodules; CT 80–100 HU; iodinated contrast vs later radioiodine | **Place** |
| ESR / radiology teaching material on endocrine imaging | US thyroid; MRI pituitary (posterior T1 bright spot); CT/MRI adrenals | **Place** |

Plain abdominal films do not show hormones. That claim is the absence of these structures on standard x-ray teaching, plus the existence of dedicated US/MRI/nuclear pathways.
