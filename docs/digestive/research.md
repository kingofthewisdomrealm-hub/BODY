## Organs

**Hollow (GI tract):** mouth → pharynx → esophagus → stomach → small intestine (duodenum, jejunum, ileum) → large intestine (cecum, appendix, colon, rectum) → anus. NIDDK, Cleveland Clinic, OpenStax. ✓

**Solid / accessory:** salivary glands, liver, gallbladder, pancreas. Teeth and tongue count as accessory mechanical organs. StatPearls GI physiology. ✓

Living alimentary canal ~**25 ft (7.6 m)**; cadaver ~**35 ft** after tone is lost. Use living. OpenStax. ✓

Small intestine ~**10 ft living**, diameter ~**1 in**; large intestine ~**5 ft**, diameter ~**3 in**. OpenStax. ✓  
Cleveland Clinic’s “22 ft small bowel” is the relaxed / cadaver-style figure. Label it if we show both. ⚠

Stomach: empty ≈ fist; can stretch toward **~4 L** in textbook extremes. Four regions: cardia, fundus, body, pylorus. Three muscle layers (oblique + circular + longitudinal). OpenStax, StatPearls. ✓

Wall, lumen → outside: mucosa, submucosa, muscularis, serosa (adventitia in the thorax). ENS: myenteric plexus in muscularis (motility), submucosal plexus (secretion). ~100 million enteric neurons. OpenStax. ✓

## Motion

**Peristalsis:** muscle behind the bolus contracts, muscle ahead relaxes. NIDDK. ✓

**Swallow:** oral phase voluntary; pharyngeal and esophageal automatic. Epiglottis covers the trachea. Upper third of esophagus skeletal muscle, lower third smooth. StatPearls. ✓

**Stomach:** propulsion → grind against a tight pylorus → **retropulsion**. Only particles **≲ 2 mm** leave during fed mixing. Leftovers swept out later by **migrating motor complexes**. StatPearls digestion. ✓

**Colon:** haustral mixing, then mass movements into rectum. Internal sphincter reflex; external sphincter voluntary. ✓

A meal **spreads**. Scintigraphy: part of a meal can enter the colon while part is still in the stomach. Colorado State / Camilleri. ✗ single cartoon pellet

## Chemistry by station

| Station | pH | Secretions | Breaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mouth | 6.7–7.0 | Saliva (~1–1.5 L/day): amylase, lingual lipase, mucus | Starch start; fat start |
| Esophagus | — | None added | Transport only |
| Stomach | ~1.5–3.5 (parietal canaliculus can be ~0.8) | HCl, pepsinogen, intrinsic factor, mucus, gastrin | Protein; gastric lipase (minor) |
| Duodenum | neutralized toward 6–7 | Bile, pancreatic HCO₃ + enzymes, enterokinase | All three macros |
| Jejunum | ~6–7 | Brush-border disaccharidases, peptidases | Bulk absorption |
| Ileum | | | B12+IF, bile salts |
| Colon | | None from “glands” in the NIDDK table | Bacteria; water; vitamin K |

Carbs **do not** digest in the stomach — amylase dies in acid. StatPearls. ✓

Endpoints: carbs → monosaccharides; protein → amino acids / oligopeptides; fat → fatty acids + monoglycerides → micelles → chylomicrons in **lacteals**. NIDDK, StatPearls. ✓

Pancreatic proteases ship as zymogens. Enterokinase → trypsin → cascade. Otherwise the pancreas digests itself. ✓

## Transit (healthy adult, mixed solid meal)

Bands, not a stopwatch. ~

| Segment | Band | Source family |
|---|---|---|
| 50% gastric emptying | 2.5–3 h | Colorado State summary |
| Stomach empty | 4–5 h typical; capsule studies ~2–5 h (some protocols 0.4–15 h) | WMC / capsule review |
| Small intestine | ~3–7 h | Capsule review 2023 |
| Colon | ~16–40 h typical; normal out to ~59 h; often quoted 30–40 h | WMC, Colorado State |
| Whole gut | ~23–37 h typical; normal out to ~73 h | Capsule review / WMC |

Fat slows gastric emptying. Liquids leave faster than solids. Stress and sex can shift colon times. ~ / ✓ direction

## What it looks like

| Stage | Look |
|---|---|
| Bolus | Cohesive, wet, still food-colored |
| Chyme | Opaque acidic slurry; gray-tan |
| Post-bile | Fat turns cloudy emulsion |
| Distal small bowel | Less volume, still not brown |
| Colon | Water out, bacteria in, **stercobilin → brown**, gas bubbles if fermentable carb leftover |
| Stool | Bristol 1–7 |

Plain **x-ray does not show the sandwich.** Bone white, gas black, fecal mottling gray. To watch a bolus you need **barium / fluoroscopy** (or CT/MRI/ultrasound/endoscopy/capsule). RadiologyInfo, StatPearls barium. ✓  
Drawing a cheeseburger on a KUB without a caption is ✗.

Bristol types (Lewis & Heaton 1997): 1 nuts → 7 watery. Form tracks transit better than frequency in their validation. ✓ scale; ◈ any one meal → one type.

## Gas

Swallowed air (N₂) + colonic fermentation (H₂, CO₂, CH₄) of carbs the small bowel missed. Merck, Monash FODMAP, StatPearls FODMAP. ✓

High-FODMAP / GOS / polyol / lactose-if-no-lactase foods raise gas in everyone; IBS adds hypersensitivity to the same stretch. Monash. ✓ mechanism, ~ symptoms

## Control

Hormones: gastrin, somatostatin, CCK, secretin, GIP, GLP-1, PYY, motilin, ghrelin. Nerves: CNS + ENS. NIDDK, StatPearls. ✓

Blood: celiac + mesenteric arteries in; **hepatic portal vein** out to the liver, not straight to the heart. ~¼ of cardiac output splanchnic while resting-and-digesting. OpenStax. ✓
